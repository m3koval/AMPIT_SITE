The short answer

AMP IT charges per user, per month. Essential is $319 per user (5-user minimum), Better is $445 and Best is $499. A 10-person office on Essential pays about $3,190 a month. A 15-person firm on Best pays about $7,500 a month.

Standard managed IT agreements are 12 months. One-time projects, like a Microsoft 365 migration or a security assessment, do not require a 12-month agreement.

What the monthly price includes

Every plan includes:

  • Remote monitoring and management, with operating system and software patching
  • 24/7 automated threat detection with managed response
  • Email security and phishing protection
  • Automated backup of computers and cloud data
  • Helpdesk support for your team
  • New hire setup and offboarding when someone leaves
  • Two-step login (MFA) enforcement and mobile device support
  • A monthly health report
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace administration, with the licensing included

Best adds a virtual CIO for planning, a 1-hour response commitment for urgent issues, a dedicated account manager, vendor management, disaster recovery planning and compliance planning. See the full plan comparison.

What changes the price

Two things: how many people need support, and which plan fits your risk. Firms that hold sensitive client data, answer insurance or client security questionnaires, or need a faster emergency response usually land on Better or Best. A small office that needs dependable support and baseline security usually starts on Essential.

How to compare quotes

When you line up quotes, ask each provider the same questions:

  • Is the price per user or per device, and what is the minimum?
  • Are Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace licenses included, or billed separately?
  • Are there per-ticket or after-hours charges?
  • Who owns security when nothing is broken?
  • How often are backups tested, and can they show you a restore?
  • What are the support hours, and what is actually promised for response time?

Support hours

Our team supports you Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 6 PM ET. Automated monitoring runs 24/7 on covered systems. The 1-hour emergency response commitment applies to the Best plan for covered urgent issues.
